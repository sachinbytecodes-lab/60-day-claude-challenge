# 📖 Project Overview

The **Prior Authorization Journey Simulator** is an interactive educational web application designed to simplify one of the most confusing processes in healthcare.

Instead of reading lengthy articles, users experience the entire Prior Authorization process through the story of **Rahul**, **Dr. Patel**, and **Priya**, learning each stage interactively.

The simulator demonstrates:

- Doctor Visit
- Prior Authorization Request
- Insurance Review
- Step Therapy
- Denial
- Appeal
- Approval
- Final Learnings

The entire experience is presented like a chat application with realistic conversations, branching choices, and a progress tracker.

## 🎭 Interactive Storytelling

- Real-world patient scenario
- Chat-style conversation

---

## 👥 Characters

- 👦 Rahul (Patient)
- 👩 Priya (Healthcare Operations Specialist)
- 🩺 Dr. Patel (Rheumatologist)

---

## 📈 Progress Tracking

- Scene Progress Bar
- Chapter Navigation
- Completion Status

---

## 🎯 User Choices

Every chapter provides two educational choices.

Example:

- Why do insurers require this?
- How long will this take?

Each choice unlocks different explanations.

---

## 💬 Dynamic Conversations

Instead of static pages, the application creates:

- Animated chat bubbles
- Narrator messages
- Doctor responses
- Patient questions
- Healthcare explanations

---

## 🔁 Restart Feature

After finishing the journey, users can restart and explore different dialogue paths.
