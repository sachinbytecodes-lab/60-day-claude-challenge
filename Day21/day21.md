## 📖 Project Overview

The **Digital Privacy Dashboard** is a premium analytics dashboard designed to visualize and estimate how digital services may contribute to an individual's online exposure.

Using a predefined dataset of commonly used applications, the dashboard generates a complete privacy intelligence report including:

* Digital Footprint Analysis
* Privacy Scoring
* Company Exposure Rankings
* Data Collection Risk Assessment
* Digital Twin Profiling
* Risk Radar Visualization
* Privacy Improvement Simulation
* Personalized Privacy Recommendations

The dashboard follows a strict distinction between:

✅ **Facts** — Services explicitly provided by the user

⚠️ **Estimates** — Behavioural, demographic, lifestyle, and risk inferences generated from usage patterns

---

## 🎯 Objective

Modern users interact with dozens of digital platforms daily but rarely understand:

* What data is being collected
* Which companies gain visibility into their behaviour
* How ecosystems combine information
* What risks arise from data aggregation

This project converts raw application usage into a privacy-focused intelligence dashboard that explains those relationships through modern visual analytics.
