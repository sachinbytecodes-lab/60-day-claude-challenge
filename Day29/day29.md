# 🚀 Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

> **An interactive Supply Chain Crisis Management Simulation built with React (CDN), HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.**

A premium single-page browser simulation where players step into the role of a **Chief Supply Chain Officer (CSCO)** and make strategic decisions during a real-world supply chain disruption.

The simulation combines **business strategy**, **supply chain management**, **leadership**, **negotiation**, and **AI-driven decision making** into one immersive learning experience.

---

# 🌐 Live Overview

Operation Lifeline is an educational business simulation that allows users to experience how executive decisions affect an entire supply chain during a crisis.

Every playthrough generates:

- 🏢 A fictional global company
- ⚠️ A unique supply chain crisis
- 🎯 Strategic decision making
- 🤝 Supplier negotiations
- 👨‍💼 CEO leadership challenges
- 🤖 AI investment planning
- 📊 Executive performance dashboard

Since every simulation is randomized, **no two playthroughs are exactly the same.**

---

# ✨ Features

## 🎮 Interactive Simulation Flow

The simulation consists of **7 major stages**.

### 1️⃣ Welcome Screen

- Premium landing page
- Modern enterprise dashboard design
- Introduction to the simulation
- One-click start

---

### 2️⃣ Random Company Generator

Generates a completely fictional company including:

- Industry
- Annual Revenue
- Manufacturing Plants
- Warehouses
- Suppliers
- Countries
- Inventory Days
- Lead Time

Each simulation creates a different organization.

---

### 3️⃣ Crisis Generator

Randomly selects a supply chain disruption such as:

- 🔥 Factory Fire
- 🌊 Flood
- 🚢 Shipping Delay
- ⚓ Port Strike
- 💻 Cyberattack
- 💰 Supplier Bankruptcy
- 🪨 Raw Material Shortage
- ⚡ Political Conflict

Each crisis contains:

- Business context
- Risk explanation
- Financial impact
- Urgency level
- Executive briefing

---

### 4️⃣ War Room

Players must select **3 out of 6** response strategies.

Examples include:

- Backup Supplier
- Air Freight
- Inventory Pooling
- Customer Communication
- SKU Reduction
- Demand Allocation

Every decision affects:

- Cost
- Inventory
- Profit
- Delivery Speed
- Customer Satisfaction

Animated dashboards immediately visualize the consequences.

---

### 5️⃣ Supplier Negotiation

A branching 4-round negotiation simulation where players negotiate:

- Trust
- Pricing
- Lead Time

Every choice changes the final negotiation score.

---

### 6️⃣ CEO Boardroom

Five executive-level business questions evaluate leadership skills.

Topics include:

- Risk Management
- Inventory Strategy
- Customer Communication
- Technology Investment
- Executive Decision Making

Immediate feedback explains **why** each answer is correct or incorrect.

---

### 7️⃣ AI Strategy

Choose two AI investments from:

- Demand Forecasting
- Inventory Optimization
- Supplier Risk Monitoring
- Warehouse Vision AI
- Procurement Copilot

The simulator predicts long-term operational improvements.

---

### 📊 Final Executive Dashboard

At the end of the simulation, the player receives a comprehensive business report including:

- Overall Crisis Score
- Leadership Score
- Negotiation Score
- Resilience
- Cost Control
- Risk Management
- Customer Satisfaction

The report also highlights:

- ✅ Best Decision
- ⚠️ Biggest Vulnerability
- 🤖 Expert Recommendation
- 📚 Lessons Learned
- 🎯 Personalized Feedback

---

# 🖥️ Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript (ES6)
- React 18 (CDN)
- Babel JSX

No backend was used.

No npm packages.

No Tailwind CSS.

Runs completely offline by opening a single HTML file.
