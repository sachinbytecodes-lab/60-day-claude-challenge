AB Talks — 60-Day Claude AI Challenge

From First Prompt to Production App

A 60-day arc of AI fundamentals, prompt engineering, and product thinking — culminating in a fully shipped capstone: AI Job-Fit Matcher.

Weeks 1–2

AI Foundations & Effective Prompting

Built the core vocabulary: how large language models reason, how to write clear and specific prompts, and how small wording changes reshape output quality — the groundwork every later skill depended on.

Weeks 3–4

Reasoning & Research Workflows

Moved from single-shot prompts to structured, multi-step reasoning — using AI to research, compare, and synthesize rather than just answer, and learning to verify rather than blindly trust output.

Weeks 5–6

Automation & Product Thinking

Shifted from "using AI to answer questions" to "using AI to build things" — automating repetitive workflows and starting to think in terms of users, requirements, and shippable scope, not just clever prompts.

Weeks 7–8

Increasingly Sophisticated AI Projects

Projects grew from isolated scripts into real applications — introducing multimodal AI (text, files, images) and knowledge systems that persist and retrieve information, not just generate it once.

Week 9

Autonomous Agents & Multi-Agent Collaboration

Explored agentic architectures — systems that plan, act, and adapt with less step-by-step hand-holding — and how multiple specialized agents can collaborate on a single larger goal.

Days 51–60 — The Capstone

AI Job-Fit Matcher: Every Skill, One Shipped Product

The full arc, converged

A 10-day sprint that used every prior skill in service of one real product: architecture design before code (Day 2), AI-integrated prompt engineering with structured output (Day 5), production debugging and dependency management (Days 4–7), security/accessibility/performance review as a discipline (Day 8), and professional documentation and release readiness (Day 9) — landing on a live, working, publicly shareable v1.0.0.
