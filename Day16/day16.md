# Day 16 – Claude Custom Skill: Stock Fundamental Research

## Objective

Create a reusable Claude Custom Skill named **stock-fundamental-research** that generates detailed fundamental analysis reports for Indian stocks, including:

- Business overview
- Financial performance
- Valuation analysis
- Ownership trends
- Risk assessment
- Peer comparison
- Investment outlook

---

## Task Steps Completed

### 1. Configure Claude

- Set Claude effort level to **Low/Medium**
- Opened **Skills** section
- Created a new **Custom Skill**
- Skill Name: `stock-fundamental-research`
- Added provided description and instructions
- Saved the skill successfully

### 2. Test the Skill

The skill was tested using:

**HCL Technologies (HCLTECH)**

The generated report included:

- Company overview
- Financial highlights
- Revenue growth analysis
- Valuation metrics
- Promoter ownership trends
- Risk factors
- Investment thesis

## Key Findings

### Positives

- Strong cash generation
- High ROCE and ROE
- Attractive dividend yield
- Stable promoter ownership
- Debt-light balance sheet

### Concerns

- Conservative FY27 guidance
- Weak near-term IT spending environment
- Impact of one-time exceptional charges
- Slower growth outlook compared to previous years

---

## Investment View

The analysis suggests that HCL Technologies remains fundamentally strong with:

- Healthy profitability
- Consistent shareholder returns
- Strong balance sheet

However, short-term growth concerns and cautious management guidance may limit near-term stock performance.

Overall, the stock appears reasonably valued relative to sector peers and may be suitable for long-term investors seeking stability and dividends.
